**Tytuł:** The Tiny Bow That Hit Like a Giant | How It Works #8

**Opis:**
A wooden longbow has to be taller than its archer. Steppe riders shot from horseback with a bow half that size, and it still flew farther. The trick: horn glued on the inside and sinew glued on the back. Horn resists squeezing, sinew resists stretching, so the short bow stores much more energy. In 1910 an old Turkish bow shot 434 m; an Ottoman archer's record of about 845 m was carved on an aiming stone in Istanbul.
