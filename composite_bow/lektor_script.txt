Small bow. Giant punch.
A wooden longbow has to be taller than you.
Now put it on a horse.
Ask the horse.
Steppe riders wanted a much shorter bow.
Same draw. Serious power.
Sounds fake?
In 1910, a man shot an old Turkish bow 434 meters.
An Istanbul archer reached 845 meters.
That is eight football pitches.
They carved it in stone. For one arrow.
How?
Wood alone can't do that.
Here's the trick:
Glue horn on the inside. Glue sinew on the back.
Horn fights squeezing. Sinew fights stretching.
Together, a spring made of animal parts.
Short enough to shoot backward. While riding away.
A simple bow dries in a week. This one needs months.
Bowyers were patient. Customers were not.
Steppe engineers. Masters of fitting in.
The longbow? Still waiting outside the stable.
