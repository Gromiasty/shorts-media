**Tytuł:** The Jar That Replaced 86 Workers | How It Works #9

**Opis:**
172 men dragging a giant statue across the desert. No wheels, no oil. So why is one guy just standing on the sled, pouring water? Dry sand piles up in front of the runners and fights back. A little water builds tiny bridges between the grains, the sand turns stiff, and the sled glides: the pulling force drops by up to half. Too much water turns it into mud. The sweet spot is 2–5% water. Egyptologists long read the painting as a ritual; physicists tested it in a sandbox in 2014.
