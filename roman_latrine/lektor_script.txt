Romans sat here shoulder to shoulder. Twenty strangers. One sponge.

The seats? Cold marble.
But the real danger came from below.
Rats climbed up the holes. They say once, an octopus.

So Romans painted Fortuna, goddess of luck, on the wall.
Pray first. Then sit.
So where did everything go?
Here's the trick:
This toilet sat on running water.
Used water from the baths ran under the seats all day.
Everything dropped in, and the water carried it to the sewer.
No pump. No button. Just gravity.
And at your feet, a second channel. Clean water.
For this. A sponge on a stick.
Historians still argue what it cleaned. The seat... or you.
Rich Romans carved their names on baths.
