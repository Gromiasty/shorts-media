## Lektor (150 słów, przy 165–175 słowach/min ok. 51–54 s)
Prompt stylu: Read this like an excited young guy telling his friends the craziest true story ever: fast, punchy, high energy, grinning.

A stick beat a machine.
Meet the crossbow. Steel bow, crank, trigger. Point and click.
Its rival? A stick of yew. And a guy who never skipped arm day.
In 1139 the Church banned both. Everyone kept shooting anyway.
Here's the trick:
The crossbow needs zero muscle. Just crank. And crank. And crank.
Three, maybe four bolts a minute.
The longbow needs everything. Around a hundred pounds of pull, on three fingers.
So English law made men practice every holiday.
Their skeletons still show it. One arm bigger than the other.
Result? Six arrows a minute. Double the machine.
Cressy, 1346. Five thousand Genoese crossbowmen.
Their big shields? Left behind with the baggage.
Then it rained. Crossbow strings soaked and stretched.
The English, they say, unstrung their bows. And hid the strings under their hats.
Verdict: the machine had a trigger. The stick had a hat. [jedyna pauza 0,4 s + świerszcze]
Which is how… → pętla do „A stick beat a machine.”

(TTS: „Cressy”; napisy: „Crécy”.)
