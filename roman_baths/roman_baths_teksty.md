## YouTube Shorts
**Tytuł:** The Roman Floor That Was Too Hot to Touch | How It Works #4

**Opis:**
Romans wore wooden sandals in the bath, because the floor was too hot. But there was no fire in the room. The trick: the whole room stood on brick stilts above a furnace (hypocaust), and hot air climbed under the floor and up through hollow tiles in the walls.
